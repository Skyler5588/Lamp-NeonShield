# How to assemble
In Onshape you will find a assembly. To assemble the shield you will need to use four M3 * 30 screws to secure the two arms to the main part. 
<img width="216" height="225" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5642ce1a-b062-4277-a814-e3d051bd3f55" />

After that put the two long arms and the two short arms into the gaps and put the neon wire through the gaps in the different arms and secure the wire by screwing the caps on with 4 M3 * 8 screws. Make sure the ends of the wire are put through the hole in the middle of the main part, and stick them on the back of the main part.
<img width="696" height="850" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1e5aa43d-1f1f-4007-864a-682a4eb76479" />

Than screw the screw the upper main part on with 4 M3 * 10 screws after gluing the center on the upper main part.
<img width="235" height="241" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/779f408d-79a4-45a5-b90b-2a55a733891c" />

FINISHED!

# BOM
you will need:
- A neon wire of 3 meters
- 4x M3*8 screws
- 4x M3*10 screws
- 4x M3*30 screws
- A IKEA GRÖNSPRÖT lamp
